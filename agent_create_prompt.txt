1. An application or underwriting case originates in Salesforce or another case management system.
2. MuleSoft APIs retrieve applicant questions and responses, applicant demographics, claim history, provider information, medical records, and other authorized business data.
3. Specialized agents retrieve and process relevant information from each source.
4. Document-processing components extract text from medical reports, scanned PDFs, prescriptions, and related files.
5. Underwriting rule retrieval identifies applicable guidelines, requirements, and decision criteria.
6. An orchestration layer coordinates agents, reconciles evidence, identifies conflicts, and prepares a case-level analysis.
7. A summarization component creates a structured underwriting case summary, including medical history, observations, potential risk factors, missing information, supporting evidence, and relevant rules.
8. A dashboard presents case-level findings with traceable supporting sources.
9. A conversational assistant answers case-specific underwriting questions by retrieving existing case evidence or invoking authorized agents and tools.
10. Human underwriters remain responsible for reviewing findings and making decisions according to company policy.

The solution should support underwriter-like reasoning by following documented business rules and analyzing evidence, but it must not invent requirements, make unsupported risk classifications, or independently authorize underwriting decisions.
